Generative AI creates new content, such as text, images, audio, video or code, instead of only classifying or predicting from existing data.

## Types of generative models

| Content | Common approach | Example uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text and code | Large language models | Drafting articles, answering questions, writing code |
| Images | Diffusion models | Illustrations, product mock-ups, image editing |
| Audio | Speech and audio models | Voice-overs, transcription, music |
| Video | Video diffusion models | Short clips, animation |

## How diffusion models create images

A diffusion model learns to remove noise from images. To create a new image, it starts from pure random noise and removes a little noise at each step, guided by your text description, until a clear image emerges.

## Using generative AI in documentation

- Draft a first version of an article from notes or a specification.
- Rewrite content for a different audience or reading level.
- Generate alt text for images and summaries for long pages.
- Translate content into other languages for review by native speakers.

## Good practice

- **Review everything.** Generated content can be inaccurate or off-brand.
- **Check rights and licences** for generated media before you publish it.
- **Disclose AI use** where your organisation or audience expects it.
- **Keep sensitive data out of prompts** unless the tool is approved for that data.
